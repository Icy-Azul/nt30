How everything works

Your daily reading
  1. On Home, tap Start today's reading, or tap any day on the Reading tracker.
  2. Tap the book buttons. They open the chapters in Gospel Library.
  3. When you finish, come back and tap I read it!
  4. Write down a thought if you'd like. It shows in the feed.
Add the app to your Home Screen
Nothing is locked

You can open any day, read ahead, or go back anytime. If you fall behind, the Reading tracker offers two ways to catch up: doubling up for a few days, or shifting your schedule.

Full or Essentials

Full covers all 260 chapters, about 9 a day. Essentials covers 89 key chapters, about 3 a day, and still tells the whole story. Switch anytime in Settings. Days you've checked off stay checked.

Social and feed

The Social page shows everyone in the challenge, how far along they are, and who read today. The feed shows when someone finishes a reading and any thoughts they share. Tap the heart to cheer someone on.

Privacy

Turn off Share my progress in Settings to hide yourself from the Social page and stop posting when you finish. Turn on Post anonymously to show "Anonymous" instead of your name in the feed, or check the box on any single thought. Thoughts only post when you choose to share them, and you can edit or delete them anytime.

Notifications

On iPhone, add the app to your Home Screen and open it from there first. Then go to Settings and tap Turn on notifications. You can choose a reminder time, or turn them off anytime.

Switching phones

Sign in with your username and password on the new phone. Your progress comes with you. Forgot your password? Text Shantz and he can reset it.
