How everything works

Your daily reading
  1. On Home, tap Start today's reading. The chapters open right in the app.
  2. At the end, tap I finished Day. That checks off the day.
  3. Prefer Gospel Library? Tap Day details, then Open in Gospel Library. When you're done, come back and tap I read it!
  4. Write down a thought if you'd like. It shows in the feed.
Add the app to your Home Screen
Reading in the app

Tap Aa at the top to change the text size, background, font, spacing, and layout. You can also turn on red letters for the words of Jesus. The small headphones button reads the chapter out loud.

Tap any verse to highlight it in one of four colors, copy it, or share it to the feed. Your highlights are saved to your account and show up in Library, then My highlights.

A faint dotted underline means others in the community highlighted that verse. Only counts are shown, never names. You can turn this off in Settings.

The Library

Open any book and chapter of the New Testament anytime. Green chapters are ones you've read. A chapter counts as read when you scroll to the end of it. Only finishing a day's reading checks off the day.

Nothing is locked

You can open any day, read ahead, or go back anytime. If you fall behind, the Daily tracker offers two ways to catch up: doubling up for a few days, or shifting your schedule.

Full or Essentials

Full covers all 260 chapters, about 9 a day. Essentials covers 89 key chapters, about 3 a day, and still tells the whole story. Switch anytime in Settings. Days you've checked off stay checked.

Community

The People tab shows everyone in the challenge, how far along they are, and who read today. The Feed shows when someone finishes a reading, plus thoughts and highlights people share. Tap the heart to cheer someone on.

Privacy

Turn off Share my progress in Settings to hide yourself from the People tab and stop posting when you finish. Turn on Post anonymously to show "Anonymous" instead of your name in the feed, or check the box on any single thought. Thoughts only post when you choose to share them, and you can edit or delete them anytime.

Notifications

On iPhone, add the app to your Home Screen and open it from there first. Then go to Settings and tap Turn on notifications. You can choose a reminder time, or turn them off anytime.

Switching phones

Sign in with your username and password on the new phone. Your progress comes with you. Forgot your password? Text Shantz and he can reset it.
